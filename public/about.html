about me

hello! I'm cerah (she/her). I am a jack-of-all-trades kind of person & collecting hobbies is my biggest hobby.

I work professionally as a web developer, however I'm not a designer. this is 100% just a place for me to goof around and have fun in ways I can't at work.

I have played in the society for creative anachronism (sca) since 2021. currently, my persona is 12th dynasty Egyptian. previously it was 14/15th century Italian. I also like to explore Minioan, Greek, and Roman fashion. I go by Hetepheres or Laurencia.
